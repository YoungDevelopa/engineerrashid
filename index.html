Independent project·Agricultural technology / AI

KUKU-PREDIKT – Poultry Health Technology

An AI-powered poultry health initiative exploring how computer vision can help farmers identify potential poultry health conditions using images of chicken droppings. The project aims to make technology-assisted poultry health support more accessible to farmers.

  • Developed an EfficientNet-B0 image classification model.
  • Worked with poultry image datasets covering coccidiosis, Newcastle disease, salmonella, healthy and other classifications.
  • Exported the trained model to ONNX for model deployment and integration.
  • Exploring practical applications in poultry production, farmer support and agricultural traceability.

An independent project in development. It is not presented as a certified veterinary diagnostic system.

ZION Mapping – Land Information & GIS Solutions

A geospatial technology initiative developing mapping and land information tools to support better land-related decisions, spatial analysis and environmental awareness in Kenya.

  • Developed ZION Land Check, a web-based land assessment and mapping tool.
  • Applied GIS concepts, spatial analysis and proximity assessment around roads, rivers, wetlands and railway infrastructure.
  • Developed interactive mapping functionality using Leaflet and Django.
  • Exploring wider applications of GIS in land management, agriculture and environmental assessment.
